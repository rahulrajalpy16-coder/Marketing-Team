## 3. Template styles (from public template galleries, third-party)

Sources: FlexClip event recap page (https://www.flexclip.com/create/event-recap-video.html), Pippit/CapCut event photo recap list (https://www.pippit.ai/templates/event-photo-recap), Renderforest recap list (https://www.renderforest.com/templates/video/recap?aspectRatio=square), nerdschalk Canva how-to (https://nerdschalk.com/make-recap-video-canva/). Canva's gallery returned 403. These pages give names and run times, not frame-level detail, so style labels below are inferred from template names (third-party, unverified) plus general knowledge of the genre.

Pippit list (40 event photo recap templates): run times from 0:08 to 0:59, most about 0:10 to 0:35 (verified from the page, observed 2026-11-06). Nerdschalk's Canva guide gives no recommended seconds per photo. FlexClip page gives no length. Renderforest lists scene counts, not durations, and marks templates premium.

Common style patterns (style patterns are free to imitate in principle; never copy a specific creator's or template's layout, wording, animation set or design):
1. Photo dump / quick-cut recap (named "Photo Dump", "Recap dump", 8 to 10 s on Pippit): rapid cuts, 0.5 to 1 s per photo is typical of the genre (general knowledge, not from pages), beat-synced music, minimal text.
2. Multi-photo grid / frame collage ("9Pic Frame", "8Pic Frame", "18Pic Clip", "Modern Collages Opener"): several photos on screen at once, frames animate in. Good for fitting many photos in short time.
3. Clip-transition recap ("12 Clips Transition", "10PicClip Transition"): one photo at a time, stylised transition between each.
4. Typography-led recap and titles (Renderforest "Clean Typography Pack", "Motion Titles Pack", "Dynamic Stomp Titles Pack"): large animated headline words between or over photos.
5. Corporate/annual recap slideshow (FlexClip "corporate year recap", "annual company report"): calm slideshow, brand colours, lower-third style captions, closing thanks card. Closest to Sinor's tone.
6. Slow-motion / cinematic recap ("Event recap slowmo", "Event vlog recap"): slower pacing, push-in motion on photos, mood-driven.
Text treatment: pages only confirm "dynamic text animations" exist (FlexClip) and text can be animated in Canva (nerdschalk). Details of fonts and timing are not given.
Photo-count note: nerdschalk workflow suggestion of 10 to 15 photos is a third-party suggestion; our set is 16 (9 JPG now usable, 7 HEIC pending).

## 5. Prioritised upgrades (ffmpeg and Pillow only, no paid software)

Tag key: [S] supported by a verified source; [O] observed in examples or template galleries (third-party); [H] hypothesis to test.

1. Add slow Ken Burns push-in or drift on every photo (ffmpeg zoompan on pre-scaled images) with 2.0 to 2.5 s per photo, replacing flat crossfades. [O] style 3 and 6 patterns. Effect on LinkedIn result: [H].
2. Rewrite the burned-in text as a short story with the thank-you message surfaced in the first 5 s: a clear title over the best photo, then 3 to 4 lower-third lines (visitors, partners, conversations at the stand) in large phone-readable type with safe margins. [S] V2 (burn-in, silent design, key content early).
3. Group partners in one clear partner card (text only, names confirmed by Rahul), mirroring the "thanks split across visitors, partners, team" pattern. [O] E2 and Al Masaood title pattern. Logos only with permission.
4. Offer a 9:16 variant (1080x1920) next to the 4:5 master; post one, do not post both. [S] V1 accepts both; which performs better is [H]. Third-party claim that immersive feed favors 9:16 is unverified.
5. Add a collage/grid frame (2x2 or 3 photos) for 1 to 2 beats so all 16 photos fit without lengthening the video. [O] style 2. Keep total near 25 to 30 s. Length effect: [H].
6. Design a cover/thumbnail PNG matching 1080x1350 (under 2 MB) in case it can be set. [S] for ads only (V1); for organic it is [H] and the option must be checked in the LinkedIn UI.
7. Companion PDF carousel (document post, up to 300 pages, 100 MB, static pages) or a native multi-photo post of up to 20 photos at 4:5 (V4, V5). This could carry all photos as a separate post later, rather than inside the same post. [S] limits only; engagement benefit is [H]. Do not claim carousels outperform video.
8. Upload an SRT as closed captions instead of, or beside, burned-in text only if the video gains speech; for a silent video with burned-in text, check that LinkedIn auto-captions do not create a duplicate track. [S] V6 (partly).
9. Music: none unless Rahul supplies a licensed track; flag as open. [H] that music improves retention.

Do not use: invented benchmarks (for example "captions raise completion by 80%", "85% sound off"), unverified algorithm claims. The 79% and 300% figures above are LinkedIn's own statements with dates and caveats, not Sinor results.

## Simple explanation
LinkedIn's own pages say to design for people watching without sound, put the main point early, and use burned-in text; 4:5 and 9:16 are both accepted. I could not open any LinkedIn post, so I have no engagement numbers for similar posts, and the ADIPEC thank-yous I could read were text articles. Template sites suggest recap videos are mostly 10 to 35 seconds with moving photos and big text. Our v3 fits the length and ratio but is static, so the best next steps are gentle motion on photos, a clearer first 5 seconds, a partner card and optionally a vertical version.
