## 4. Recommendation (design judgement, hypothesis to test, not sourced)

Keep Sinor's DP Capability Profile look: deep navy page, cyan accent, white photo panels with thin frame, small uppercase cyan section labels, title plus one-line description, brand chips at card foot, thin header with logo, footer with company name and website. Logo is opaque: place it on a white or light header chip, or obtain the white variant, and check on navy.

Budget (about 40 pages, A4 portrait): 1 cover, 1 inside note and disclaimer, 2 contents and index, about 6 to 8 section dividers or combined intro pages, 18 package systems on 9 spreads (about 18 pages), about 70 items on about 14 pages at 6 per page (plus 1 to 2 spare), 1 part-number index, 1 back cover. Roughly 38 to 42 pages. Package spreads are the main page cost; if over budget, put 2 compact packages per page.

Item-card page (6 per page, 2 columns x 3 rows). Each card: white framed photo panel on top (about 45 percent of card height, product on neutral white, consistent crop and angle), cyan uppercase category label, bold title, one-line description, 3 to 4 key specs as label/value pairs in a small two-column table (large enough to read at about 8 pt in print), part or model number in a monospace-style chip, brand chip at foot. Reason for 6 per page: 70 items would need about 12; at 8 per page text drops too small for phones (hypothesis, test by viewing a PDF on a phone).

Cover: full-bleed navy, one large hero marine or bridge photo with navy gradient, title "Product Catalogue 2027" (year to confirm), cyan label line "DP, position reference, automation, spares", logo on light chip, website at foot.

Contents: one page, sections with page numbers and a cyan number column; tappable links in the PDF so phone users can jump. Second page: part-number and model A to Z index with page numbers.

Section divider: navy full page, large cyan section number, section title, a 3 to 5 line scope note, small list of what is inside. Use for: Position references, DP and control, Automation and electrical, Spares, Package systems.

Package-system spread (one page each, or 2 per spread): left: framed hero image or simple block diagram; right: package name, one-paragraph intended use, numbered component list as a table (item no., description, qty, part or model, page link to its item card), and a "Ask Sinor to confirm configuration" CTA. Components link back to item cards, so specs are not repeated.

Back cover: navy, logo, contact block (to be supplied by Rahul), website, QR code to enquiry (only if approved), and the disclaimer.

Disclaimer (draft wording for Rahul and legal to approve, not copied from any source): "All product names, logos and brands are trademarks of their respective owners and are used for identification only. Sinor Marine Services Middle East FZCO is not the manufacturer of these products unless stated, and listing does not imply endorsement or authorisation by the owner. Specifications are indicative, taken from manufacturer information, and may change without notice; confirm against current datasheets before ordering." Authorised-partner status must be stated per brand only after Rahul confirms it.
