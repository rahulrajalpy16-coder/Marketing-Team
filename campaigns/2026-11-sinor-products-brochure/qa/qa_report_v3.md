Reviewer: qa-reviewer (independent of the producer). Date: 2026-11-11.

## Verdict: PASS WITH DEFECTS (0 blocker, 10 major, 14 minor). Not ready to send until the majors are fixed.

## Verification scope (what I really did)
- Rendered all 29 pages at 80 dpi and looked at every page. Two crops (p4, p11) at 160 dpi to check word breaks and the support strip. Small print cannot be fully judged at 80 dpi, so type sizes were taken from the CSS in brochure_v3.html (pt values), not measured on the PDF.
- Compared all 16 trees (144 leaves) with evidence_trees.md and grepped register_text.txt, results/*.json for leaves not listed in evidence_trees.md.
- Compared all 47 cards programmatically against results/*.json and extra_items.json (description text, spec rows, legacy flag), and read 20 of them against their evidence notes.
- Viewed a labelled contact sheet of 19 leaf and hero photos; checked image_credits.csv for source type. I did not inspect each photo at full size for tiny watermarks.
- Text checks on brochure_v3.txt: dashes (0 em or en), contacts, emails, spellings, customer or price words.
- NOT done: no print proof, no check of the Type 3 font warnings from pdftoppm (cosmetic, no visible effect), no independent web check of any spec (evidence files only), no check that part numbers are right beyond the evidence_trees.md lines.

## Check results by task item

1. Plain-language test. PARTIAL. The tree layout works: system on top, five branches, icon plus one plain line per leaf, "Choose the level of backup you need" tiles on the DP pages, "What it does" lines in everyday words. A manager can follow DP-01 and PR-01 in about 30 seconds. It is weakened by V3-03 (broken words), V3-05 (jargon), V3-10 (type size), V3-01 and V3-02 (wrong statements) and V3-06 (misplaced branch). Connector lines are clear. Half-page trees are easy to read. Group names are plain.
   Five most useful readability fixes: (1) stop mid-word breaks, shorten leaf names; (2) raise leaf notes to 7.5 pt or more in brighter text; (3) add a one-page glossary and expand DP, USBL, GNSS, FMEA on first use on every page; (4) show the "We can support you with" strip on all 16 trees as plain chips, worded "depending on the system"; (5) replace placeholder cards and the same-photo-different-item thumbnails with neutral icons or real photos.
2. Trees vs evidence. 16 trees, 144 leaves. Almost all leaves are supported by evidence_trees.md or the register text; part numbers (20-0310, 38-0037-4, 38-0038-4, 20-0252, 20-0306, 20-0253, 20-0337, 33-0365-3, 20-0276-BLU, Type 8142, Type 8223, 603-0230, 620-0770, 603-0181) match the evidence lines. Not supported: Veripos correction subscription (V3-09). Wrong note: Ex tablet (V3-02). Unsupported claim: DP class 1 to 3 (V3-01). Inferred only: Artemis main build assembly note, HT CO3 "newer" (V3-11, V3-13). Gaps listed in V3-12 to V3-14.
3. Pages. All 29 viewed. Page numbers in the footer, contents (p3, p6, p17, p20, p25, p28), section openers and the item index match the real pages (DP-01 p4, DP-02 p5, PR-01 p7, PR-02 p8, PR-03 and 04 p9, PR-05 and 06 p10, PR-07 p11, PR-08 p12, PR-09 and 10 p13, PR-11 and 12 p14, PR-13 and 14 p15, parts cards p16, SN p18 to 19, CA p21 to 24, SP p26 to 27). ## Candidate lessons for the Director
- Narrow multi-column trees need a word-length check: run a script that finds any word longer than the branch can hold before rendering.
- Strip or footer lines hidden by CSS on some page variants should be checked on every variant.
- A leaf photo must be matched to the leaf name, not to the system hero; use an icon where only a family photo exists.
- For client copy, ask whether "Ex", "DP class" or similar technical tags are correct before turning them into plain notes.
