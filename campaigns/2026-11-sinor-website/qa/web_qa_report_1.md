Date: 2026-11-07. Reviewer: web-qa-reviewer (independent; I did not build the site). Site: campaigns/2026-11-sinor-website/site/ (6 HTML pages, 2 CSS, 2 JS, robots.txt, sitemap.xml, no images, 152 KB).

## Verdict

0 FAIL, 5 WARN, 9 NOTE. The build is technically sound: no horizontal overflow at 320, 375, 768, 1440 or 667x375, no console errors, zero external requests, all 27 internal links and anchors resolve, hero works at all four scroll beats and in both static fallbacks, form never claims sending. The open issue the builder named is REAL (W1). The main publish blockers are content decisions, not code (W3, W4, W5).

## What was verified

Headless Chromium on Linux only, Playwright, my own local static server. Viewports 320x640, 375x667, 375x812, 667x375, 768x1024, 1440x900 (plus the script's 375, 768, 1440). Scroll positions, keyboard sequences, reduced motion and no-JS contexts emulated by Playwright. Pointer events were synthetic mouse events from Playwright.

## What was NOT verified

- Real iPhone or Android devices, real touch input, mobile browser chrome and the on-screen keyboard (W1 is shown with a synthetic mouse only).
- Safari (WebKit) and Firefox (Gecko) rendering and behaviour; Playwright's other engines were not run.
- Screen readers (VoiceOver, TalkBack, NVDA, JAWS). Live region behaviour, announcement of the error summary and the "Not sent" panel are unheard.
- A full manual WCAG 2.2 audit. Only the items listed above were checked; focus ring contrast, non-text contrast, forced colours, 200 percent zoom and text spacing overrides were not tested.
- Real-user performance. The script's LCP and CLS numbers are local, unthrottled, indicative only.
- Hosting, HTTPS, headers, caching, the real domain, 404 behaviour on a real host.
- Contrast of non-hero text beyond the script's DOM-based check (it skips background images and ignores semi-transparent backgrounds); I sampled pixels only for the hero.
- Document reading: I read brain files, the copy/home.md file in full and the other copy files programmatically (matching every rendered line and every slot line, plus targeted greps), seo-spec.md and fact_check.md by targeted search plus the sections cited, and build-notes.md in full. I did not read brief/brief.md, design/wireframes.md, design/sitemap.md, design/a11y-spec.md and design/build-decisions.md end to end; items they require that I did not test one by one are therefore unchecked.

## Candidate lessons for the Director

1. Full-page screenshots of a sticky, scroll-linked hero are misleading (stacked captions); the QA script should also capture viewport shots at scroll 0, 33, 66, 100 percent for pages with a pinned hero.
2. Test pointer-sequence defects (mouse down, reflow, mouse up) with real mouse down and up; fill() and click() hide them. Reserve space for inline errors by design.
3. A grid container that holds a bare button stretches it to full width; test buttons at all widths, not only labels.
4. Content gates (Q1, Q4 in fact_check.md) survive into rendered copy unless the builder is told to mark or drop them; list open gate items in the build brief with a decision for each.
