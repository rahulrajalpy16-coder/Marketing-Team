Dynamic Positioning

Dynamic Positioning (DP) is a computer-based control system that automatically maintains a vessel's position and/or heading using its thrusters. It lets a vessel hold position without anchors or mooring lines.

Sinor Marine Technologies works on DP systems as part of its marine and offshore technology focus.

Reference systems

A DP system needs to know where the vessel is. Position reference systems provide that measurement. Different types work on different principles and suit different conditions. Several types are often used together.

The company works on reference systems alongside DP.
