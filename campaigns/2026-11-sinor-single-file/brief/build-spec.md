## Design direction
- Modern, minimal, BIG type, image and video led. Dark cinematic navy stage (follow the style study tokens in /home/user/Marketing-Team/campaigns/2026-11-style-cinematic-dark/design/tokens.css and the DNA in references/design-dna.md there: near-black stage, one luminous focal object, huge short headline, tiny quiet nav, small pill buttons, one soft glow). Colours: abyss #050A12, deep #0A1626, surface #101F33, raise #16293F, text #F2F6FA, muted #A9B8C9, accent #5BA4E0, accent-hi #8CC4F2, steel #7F9BB8, warm glow #F2A65A (sparing). Body text at least 4.5:1 contrast; focus ring #8CC4F2 visible; tap targets 44px; honour prefers-reduced-motion; one h1 per page.
- Fonts (embedded files already in src/fonts): Outfit variable for headings (weights 600 to 800, tight tracking, sizes up to clamp(3rem, 11vw, 10rem)), DM Sans variable for body. Declare with @font-face url(fonts/....woff2) in css/site.css (the build inlines them) and system-font fallbacks.
- Logos in src/media/logo/: logo-mark-white.png (ellipse mark, transparent), logo-full-white.png (mark + wordmark, transparent), plus blue originals. Header: white mark + the text SINOR MARINE TECHNOLOGIES (HTML, letter-spaced caps). Footer and About: full white logo. Always transparent, never on a white box.
- More real media, fewer drawn illustrations: every product page opens with a full-bleed image or video band; Home uses large full-bleed video bands (clips) and image grids; the Products hub cards use the real images; Simulator page uses the clips and the film. Keep the canvas diagram scenes only where they explain something (DP architecture diagrams, partner network map, service network).
- Media available (src/media/): img/*.jpg (simulator stills; s-series already cropped to remove a menu strip; c_*.jpg are poster frames of the clips), clip/dp, hpt, onedp, radar, see, sprint, taut, thruster .mp4 (720 wide, no audio, 9 to 15 s each), clip/film.mp4 (the 3 minute simulator film, with audio; use a normal controls video, preload none, with c_see.jpg as poster). The clips contain burned-in caption text at the top of the frame, so show them uncropped at the top (object-position top, or contain).
- Asset rule: EVERY reference to a file under media/ in JavaScript or generated HTML goes through A('media/...') from js/assets.js (resolves blob URLs in the single file). Videos: muted loop playsinline, play only when in view (IntersectionObserver), preload none or metadata.

## HERO (Home)
- Left: words and everything appear progressively. The h1 (the old site's: "Hold the vessel where it needs to be.") appears word by word (stagger, rise and fade); then the support line and two pill buttons (Explore products, Try the simulator); then, as the visitor scrolls the runway, the old site's other three captions come in one after another (each with its h2-size line and one support line): "Built to keep working." / "From bridge to boat deck." / "Across Asia and Oceania." with their old support lines. All text is real DOM text. Scroll cue at the bottom.
- Right: a 3D Marine Technologies DP console (the real twin-station console seen in the old media: two angled screens, joystick panel with a red emergency button, cabinet below) in a WebGL canvas that ROTATES AND MOVES AS THE VISITOR SCROLLS, like the rotating 3D product in the sample the owner liked (a phone that turns as you scroll). Sticky stage 100svh inside a runway about 420svh tall (desktop) / 380svh (phone). On a phone the 3D sits in the upper or lower half of the stage and the text in the other half; nothing overlaps text. Visible small caption: "Bridge Mate DP console, 3D illustration". The 3D is an illustration, not the product CAD.
- Module contract (hero3d.js is written by the 3D agent, the page agent calls it): window.SinorHero3D.create({ canvas, reducedMotion }) returns null if WebGL is unavailable, else a controller { setProgress(p) /* 0..1 */, resize(), start(), stop(), dispose() }. The page calls start()/stop() when the hero enters/leaves view, resize() on resize, and setProgress(p) once per animation frame with the runway scroll progress.
- Fallback (no WebGL, reduced motion, or if create() returns null): show the real still media/img/s70.jpg in the right panel (cover, rounded) with no scroll linkage, and show all text immediately without word animation.
