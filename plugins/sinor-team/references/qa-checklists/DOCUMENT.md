# QA Checklist: Documents, Reports, Spreadsheets, Presentations

- W1 File opens. For PDF: `pdftotext` extracts text and `pdftoppm` renders pages. For DOCX/XLSX/PPTX: unzip parses.
- W2 Structure matches the requested or approved template.
- W3 Headings, numbering, page numbers and tables are consistent; nothing clipped (render pages to PNG and look).
- W4 Spreadsheet formulas recalculate to the stated values.
- W5 Spelling of names, vessels, part numbers checked against inputs.
