# QA Checklist: Email and Correspondence

- E1 Recipient, salutation and subject match the instruction.
- E2 Purpose stated in the first sentence.
- E3 Every date, time, vessel name, location and number matches the instruction exactly (character for character).
- E4 No commitments (price, delivery, compatibility) the user did not authorise.
- E5 Length and tone match any explicit instruction, otherwise applicable preferences. Measure words with `sinor.py lint`.
- E6 Clear next step or ask.
- E7 It is a draft. Nothing was sent.
